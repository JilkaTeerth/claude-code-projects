# The brief template

What to actually send when you want a reel. The original ask, *"make a reel from this
folder"*, leaves the editor guessing on audience, format, length, names, and what
"good" means, and guessing produces a reel you then have to describe your way out of.

Fill in the blanks. Anything you genuinely don't care about, write "your call", that's
a decision too, and an explicit one.

---

```
Cut an the academy reel.

FOOTAGE   <folder path>
FORMAT    <name + number from reel-formats.md, or "pick one and tell me why">
LENGTH    <seconds>
FOR       <who's watching: parents / prospective students / the players themselves>
FEELING   <one word: pride, hunger, belonging, adrenaline, warmth>
POSTING   <Instagram Reels / Stories / YouTube Shorts>
AUDIO     <trending audio added in-app / generate a bed / real audio only>
NAMES     <player and category names, exactly as they should be spelled, or "none">
MUST INCLUDE  <sponsor beats, a specific photo, a specific line of copy>
DEADLINE  <when>

Before building: show me the beat sheet and the frame total.
After building: export, verify with ffprobe, and show me the hook frame,
a middle frame, and the last frame.
```

---

## Why each line is there

- **FORMAT**, the single highest-leverage field. "A reel" has sixteen answers in
  `reel-formats.md` and they produce completely different videos. If you don't know, say
  "pick one and tell me why", that's a real instruction and gets you a reasoned choice
  instead of a default.
- **FOR / FEELING**, these decide pacing, music, and whether text is hype or quiet. A
  reel for parents and a reel for the players are not the same cut of the same footage.
- **AUDIO**, the most common re-do. Most Reels get trending audio added in-app, which
  means the export should be a clean visual cut with no baked music. Say which you want
  up front.
- **NAMES**, never let the editor read a name off a jersey and burn it in. A misspelled
  junior player's name on a public post is the mistake that actually gets noticed.
- **The two "show me" lines**, they convert "it's done" into something you can check in
  ten seconds without opening the file.

## Fastest useful version

If you only write one line, write this one:

> Cut a 20s Format 2 Podium Ladder from `<folder>` for parents, feeling = pride, no baked
> music (I'll add trending audio). Beat sheet first.

## Iterating on a cut

Reference beats by frame or by what's on screen, and change one variable at a time:

- "The third still holds too long, take it to 50 frames."
- "Hook isn't landing. Try the exhaustion photo instead and re-export."
- "Grade is too warm on the blue wall shots. Pull temperature back 200K."
- "Text collides with the logo on the end card. Move it down."

Palmier keeps the project open, so re-exporting after a tweak is one call, not a rebuild.
