# NICHE.md, Format Bible: "What If You Lived As..."

## Core concept
Immersive, second-person stories that drop the viewer inside the life of an animal, extinct creature, microscopic organism, historical figure, profession, or object. The viewer feels like they live an entire lifetime in one video. Not a list of facts, a survival story the viewer *inhabits*.

## Why this niche compounds
Infinite topics, high retention through storytelling, cross-age curiosity, educational without feeling like a lesson, serializable (same skeleton, new life every time), and binge-friendly. One good format → hundreds of videos.

## The skeleton (every episode)
1. **Hook (0 to 8s)**, a line that transports instantly + the biggest threat or stakes. No greeting.
2. **Chapter 1, Birth**, where you're born, what you are, first instincts, first threats, your odds.
3. **Chapter 2, Childhood**, learning to survive, food, social behavior, milestones, danger.
4. **Chapter 3, Adult life**, daily rhythm, strengths, weaknesses, competition, relationships.
5. **Chapter 4, The biggest challenge**, the central conflict. Winter, a predator, humans, collapse.
6. **Chapter 5, Legacy**, how it ends, offspring, impact, what happens after.
7. **Ending**, one memorable, slightly haunting takeaway.

## Voice (non-negotiable)
- Second person only: "You / Your / Now."
- Never: "the animal," "this species," "scientists say."
- The viewer never leaves the role.

## Tone
Cinematic, fast-paced, emotional, immersive, documentary-style, slightly dramatic, but factually honest. Dark and eerie enough to reward "just one more minute."

## Cadence rules (for retention)
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Open a curiosity loop every 60 to 90 seconds ("But something is already hunting you." / "You don't know it yet, but you will only live six months."). Pay every loop off.
- Rising stakes into Chapter 4.
- Land the ending on a single clean line.

## Length targets
- Long-form: 8 minutes (this system's default) ≈ 1,200 to 1,400 spoken words.
- Shorts spin-off: 60 to 90 seconds ≈ 150 to 220 words, using only Hook + Chapter 4 + Ending.

## Visual style
Locked to `templates/MASTER_IMAGE_PROMPT.md`: crude, rushed, MS-Paint-style hand drawings on plain white. This deliberately-bad style is the channel's signature, the contrast between eerie narration and childlike drawings is the hook. Do NOT switch to realistic/cinematic AI renders.

## Topic bank (endless, pull from here)
**Animals:** wolf, eagle, orca, honeybee, ant, octopus, blue whale, tiger, polar bear, komodo dragon, emperor penguin, arctic tern, angler fish, mantis shrimp, vampire bat, naked mole rat.
**Extinct:** T-Rex, velociraptor, mammoth, dodo, saber-toothed cat, megalodon, trilobite, Neanderthal.
**Microscopic:** red blood cell, white blood cell, virus, bacterium, neuron, tardigrade, sperm cell.
**Deep sea (rich, eerie):** anglerfish, giant isopod, vampire squid, blobfish, gulper eel, hydrothermal vent tubeworm.
**Historical / roles:** Roman gladiator, medieval plague doctor, lighthouse keeper, Pompeii citizen, WWI carrier pigeon.

## Curiosity-gap title patterns
- "What If You Were Born As the ___ Nobody Survives?"
- "You Have 6 Months to Live. You're a ___."
- "The ___ That Shouldn't Be Alive"
- "You Wake Up 4,000 Meters Underwater"
- "Everything Wants to Eat You. You're a ___."
