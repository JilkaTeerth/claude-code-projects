# Sports academy sponsorship programme (client work, anonymised)

A sponsorship programme for a squash and padel group in Pune, designed to bring in sponsorship income separate from student fees.

- Tiered sponsor packages with a surface allocation map: every physical and digital spot a sponsor's logo can go.
- When outreach showed about 70% of likely sponsors were Indian nutrition and FMCG brands competing for 2 slots, the category was split into 20 non competing sub slots.
- The pitch deck and rate card are generated from one Python rates file, so prices are never edited by hand and the two can't disagree.
- An outreach tracker page and email drafts.
- A design system that unifies the group's three brands in one blue, sampled from an existing logo.

No files here: the pricing is the client's.
